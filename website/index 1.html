The Model

Equity-based co-founding.
Not consulting. Not investing.

Siaxen takes a stake in the venture's outcome. We invest expertise, operational leadership, and synchronized architecture for equity. Our return depends entirely on the venture's success.

PRINCIPLE 01

Equity, not fees

We do not invoice. We take equity. This permanently aligns our interests with the venture's outcome. When the venture succeeds, we succeed. No other model creates this level of commitment.

Unlike consultancies that profit regardless of outcome.

PRINCIPLE 02

Co-founding, not advising

We are embedded operational leaders, not external advisors. We build alongside founders through regulatory submissions, clinical evidence, investor conversations, and market entry decisions.

Unlike advisory models that end when the engagement ends.

PRINCIPLE 03

Indefinite, not time-bounded

MedTech ventures require 3 to 7 years to reach market. Our commitment matches this reality. We remain embedded through the phases where synchronized architecture proves its value.

Unlike accelerator programs that conclude after 12 weeks.
